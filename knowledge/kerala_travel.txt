Kerala Travel Knowledge

Munnar

Munnar is a hill station in Kerala known for tea plantations, scenic viewpoints, waterfalls, and trekking opportunities.

Important places include Eravikulam National Park, Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, Tea Museum, and Lockhart Gap.

Nature activities include trekking, tea plantation walks, sightseeing, and wildlife observation.

Adventure activities include trekking, jeep tours, boating, and ziplining.

Wayanad

Wayanad is known for forests, waterfalls, wildlife, caves, and trekking.

Important places include Edakkal Caves, Soochipara Falls, Banasura Sagar Dam, and Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary.

Nature activities include waterfall visits, wildlife observation, forest exploration, and trekking.

Adventure activities include trekking, ziplining, camping, and boating.

Thekkady

Thekkady is known for Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, forests, wildlife, and boating.

Popular activities include wildlife observation, bamboo rafting, trekking, and boat cruises.

Budget Travel

Budget travelers can consider public buses, budget hotels, hostels, and local restaurants.

Using public transportation can reduce transportation costs compared with private taxis.

Travel Considerations

Kerala has different geographical regions including coastal areas, plains, and hill stations.

Travel between hill stations can take several hours because of winding mountain roads.

Weather conditions should be considered before selecting outdoor activities.

Travelers should check local activity availability, entry requirements, and weather conditions before visiting attractions.
